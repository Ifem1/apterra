## Phase boundary / explicit non-goals

No token, identity registry, leaderboard, custodial escrow, external bridge, real refund processor, marketplace, or Phase 2 integration is implemented or implied. Studio Dev addresses and transactions, once obtained, are ephemeral preview evidence. No production-agent identity or TEE attestation is claimed.

## Source reconciliation

The compendiums define capability-specific, version-bound underwriting, evidence, deterministic warrants, and downstream enforceability; they do not treat a general score or generic badge as the product. The refund-policy flow remains one vertical slice. The rows above explicitly mark the missing breadth, browser/live proof, and external approvals instead of calling the slice complete.
