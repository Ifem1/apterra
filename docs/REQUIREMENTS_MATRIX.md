## Source reconciliation notes

- Both sources define APTERRA as semantic capability underwriting that turns demonstrated, version-bound capability into scoped, expiring, enforceable authority. Neither reduces the product to a leaderboard, identity registry, generic safety badge, or payment rail.
- The rebuilt compendium is the latest presentation, but the original’s named workflows, threat controls, UX, tests, business boundary, and submission checklist remain requirements unless explicitly identified as future roadmap.
- The canonical demo requests up to $5,000 and a 90-day operating window; the risk policy grants LIMIT $100 for 14 days and CERTIFY up to the request for at most 90 days. A separate human approval override above $100 is not implemented: the current contract safely rejects amounts above the warrant ceiling.
- The MVP explicitly uses synthetic/public evidence and a disclosed harness. A content hash commits to bytes; it does not attest who generated or executed them. No TEE/provider attestation is claimed or specified as a Phase 1 requirement.
- No frontend, unit, AST-linter, or documentation result is evidence of a GenLayer consensus transaction. Live evidence must be a finalized 61997 transaction plus canonical readback.
